A queue shrinking through merges is not evidence of a fix. The chart is bounded to the export, so its right edge cannot tell us whether reports stopped afterwards.

Proposed replacement for subject-only merging

Decision 0001 and the triage page authorize matching normalized subjects without reading the body. Their review dates are stale under the knowledge base’s own rule. Keep the accepted record intact; this proposed replacement needs desk approval before it supersedes that policy.

  1. Read the report and identify the tenant, locale, booking form and entry channel. The browser’s intake tag is a guess, not the tenant’s configuration.
  2. Compare the underlying cause, not just the subject or the symptom. Different subjects can describe the same cause; identical subjects can hide different causes.
  3. Merge only when the cause is established and matches. Keep parser and hint reports apart, retain report IDs and count each ID once across overlapping export pages.
  4. With no tenant or insufficient evidence, leave the report unclassified and investigate. “Unknown” is not a shared cause and is not permission to merge.
  5. Recheck the live queue before splitting or closing a ticket. Link the replacement tickets and updated guidance once the desk approves them; do not invent ticket URLs.

This classifier covers the supplied date incident, not every product the desk supports. For other symptoms, establish the cause instead of forcing a parser or hint label.
